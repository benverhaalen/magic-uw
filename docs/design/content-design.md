## Current copy and label constraints

App-authored UI copy must never contain em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon or concise separate label as appropriate. Do not silently edit a quoted source or raw title to make a literal scan pass: preserve that evidence, and use a distinct concise display projection where needed. Visible product name is **My Magic UW**.

Course labels must be audited through source → structured metadata → display projection → actual UI. Preserve raw title, account scope and course ID; prefer verified structured code/title and targeted parsing with a safe fallback. Model assistance is an option when needed, not authority to invent course facts or merge similarly named courses. Ellipsis alone does not normalize a title. Inspect real messy examples, missing metadata and same-name courses across accounts before adopting a formatter.

Empty-state copy distinguishes a checked saved schedule from missing, incomplete and stale coverage. A healthy Home day with no timed commitments needs only a small message; recovery actions appear only when they have a real job. All-day entries and deadlines remain visible. This does not prescribe an empty full Calendar page.

Setup readiness and Connected accounts summarize the student's usable course coverage before raw connector checks. Use verified current enrollment when available; a Canvas inclusion choice alone identifies a site included in a read, not a current class. Catalog history, linked-file budgets, hidden or unpublished course areas, and account-wide optional endpoints remain inspectable behind bounded disclosure. A failed or empty endpoint never proves an instructor does not use Canvas. Name sign-in and important course-list failures near the summary, but do not call a course fully read when a relevant check is incomplete or inaccessible. This September 27 correction addresses a real saved capture with hundreds of source rows; the counts and files stay private.

## Produce the useful information before styling it

Use this path in a generation brief and review the resulting content, not merely the presence of these instructions:

**Source facts + labeled inferences → selected information → sentences and links → visible hierarchy → evidence inspection or useful action.**

1. **Assemble candidate meaning.** Keep object identity, relevant excerpt/location, source version, capture time, event/deadline time, conflicting claims and known capabilities. State separately what the sources say and what the system infers. Unknown coverage is not absence. Exact dates, identifiers and time calculations belong in code.
2. **Choose by consequence, timing, newness and evidence confidence.** Ask what changes the student's understanding or next useful step now. Consider the cost of overlooking it, when it matters, what materially changed and how well the claim is supported. A serious uncertain conflict can outrank a fresh minor update; expose the uncertainty. An unchanged requirement can remain important. Neither recency alone nor an invented numerical confidence establishes priority. Keep a short selection reason in development evidence, not as mandatory UI copy.
3. **Assign one primary job.** On Home, Briefing explains implications, preparation and changes; Today supplies timing; Upcoming prepares graded work; Study & Learn starts a selected learning activity. An object can recur when the second appearance supplies a distinct benefit. Do not repeat its summary and action to fill another region. Elsewhere, derive jobs from that page's entry and user intent rather than copying Home.
4. **Write the information the student would otherwise have to find.** Name the actual object, requirement or change, its relevant condition, and consequence when supported. A heading must reveal useful information. “Check the requirements” merely delegates the work back. Do not invent an implication to make ordinary metadata sound insightful.
5. **Expose the needed depth.** Primary text carries the decision-changing condition, deadline or uncertainty. Secondary detail carries supporting explanation, source age and evidence access where those do not change interpretation. Material uncertainty belongs beside the claim. Group related facts by meaning; allow a longer paragraph or several groups when needed. Brevity is removal of repetition, not an arbitrary sentence count.

## Links, actions and honest state

A **noun link** identifies an exact lecture, assignment or resource and opens useful context. An **evidence control** exposes support, freshness and limits. A **verb label** describes a next step, such as “Review assigned readings,” and reaches that material. Do not use “Canvas” as the action when the student needs a particular reading. Keep these roles distinct even when they share styling. Link the first useful mention; do not add prose just to display a link.

On Home, place source-shaped work actions to the right near their claim. Reserve space only when a real action exists; informational prose receives the full width. No forced CTA, checkbox or card per insight. Labels must match available capability: opening a source does not mean a generated exercise or external workflow exists.

Keep **observed source state**, **inference**, **student self-report** and **learning evidence** separate. Opening a reading proves an opening. A reported reading is reported read. A verified submission proves submission, not understanding. Preserve assistance and uncertainty in learning evidence; do not invent mastery or readiness from grades. A handled issue persists through wording changes; material new evidence can reopen it with a reason and correction path.

## Verify the actual output and continuity

For the changed generator or surface, retain a compact trace from each consequential sentence to its input and inference, selection reason, destination and visible state. Evaluate a normal entry plus relevant dense, long, no-action, stale and conflicting cases. In dense content, preserve meaningful grouping and access to remaining items; never silently truncate a critical condition or shrink text to fit.

Inspect the rendered first screen: can a student identify what matters, why it matters and what the link will do without narration? Follow one source/action and return. Refresh evidence while reading: update affected facts without jumping scroll, moving focus, losing a draft or reshuffling the active selection. A new summary alone cannot erase confirmations. A failed capture cannot replace saved work with “nothing due.”

For course detail, chat and My UW, transfer evidence discipline, meaningful hierarchy and predictable links; select content for that surface's actual task. For unrelated products, treat reduced assembly effort and information-bearing copy as provisional taste lessons, revalidated against their audience. Home's columns, right alignment and student priorities remain local requirements. Record whether these checks were specified, demonstrated with fixtures or verified in the real journey; a content contract alone demonstrates none of the latter.
