## Palette evidence and unresolved risk

A deterministic sRGB calculation of the seed values found these sampled text/background ratios: primary ink on ivory 14.62:1; muted ink 4.80:1; link 6.45:1; review gradient endpoints 5.72–8.42:1; principal identity endpoints 5.15–9.72:1; study endpoints 6.05–7.14:1. Endpoint samples do not prove all composed gradients, overlays or interaction states pass.

**Unresolved:** light shell ink over the raw brightest ember stop is 3.43:1, below the normal-text target. The focus blue directly against the darker review fill is also below 3:1. These are actual seed-pair risks, not measured failures at every rendered text location. Tokens now carry warnings; foundations require checking composed positions, keeping highlight away from small text or darkening beneath it, and offsetting filled-control focus onto the quiet workspace. The approved image was not silently recolored. Candidate warning/error/disabled roles need a rendered comparison before adoption.

## What “ready” means here

Ready to guide the next scoped design task: governing principles, source authority, visual reference, type/color/boundary roles, interaction contracts, product-surface status and review method are connected. **Not ready to claim a fully validated universal component library.** Fonts are still private, responsive policies are proposals, and implementation accessibility/behavior must be demonstrated in the actual framework.

Next validation, when authorized: one complete unfamiliar-surface journey from these inputs, including error and return, followed by a website-specific transfer. Evaluate actual artifacts and student effort; model agreement or image similarity alone is insufficient. This remains a future validation step after Ben's explicit system-only scope correction.
