# Badger BuildFest 2026: the rules that shape our submission

**Status:** researched. Read from the live site (buildfest.project.wiscweb.wisc.edu: Tracks & Awards, FAQ, Schedule, Mentors & Judges) on 2026-09-26. It complements the team's [BuildFest context](../buildfest.md).

## Deadlines and format
- **Build window:** Saturday 11:00 → **submissions close Sunday 2026-09-27 at 11:00 AM.**
- **Submit:** the repository, a two-minute project video, and the required project responses.
- **Track judging:** preliminary screening selects up to five finalists per track, who **demo live 1–3 PM Sunday.** Judges pick first and second place. Awards are announced 3:30–4:00 PM.
- **Challenges** are judged **asynchronously from the submitted materials,** with no live demo. Winners are announced 2026-09-30 at the Tech Exploration Lab's Four-week Sprint kickoff. Teams don't need to be present.
- **Awards:** at most three per team (a track plus two challenges), judged separately.

| **The Art of the Break (Agentic Stress Test)** (Tech Exploration Lab) | $500, plus the team named in a published industry report | "Stress-test your own AI agent and file a one-page Break Card covering a failure specific to how you built it, how often it happened, what you tried to stop it and what you learned." **Every card feeds into a published report on how student-built AI agents fail,** shared with industry partners | see the Break Card strategy (kept local until any findings are fixed) |

**The challenge rule:** "Your project must meaningfully address each challenge's criteria; simply using a partner tool or API is not enough." Teams can enter at most two challenges. **Open Venture** (Strato VC; market signal, founder-market fit, differentiation, path beyond the weekend) would also fit our [business model](business-model.md), but taking it means dropping one of the two current challenges. That's a team choice.

## Other rules
- **Teams:** 2–5 current UW–Madison students.
- **IP:** "You do." Teams keep ownership. The DoIT Student Innovation Hub is opt-in afterwards.
- **Opening slides** (per the team's notes): new product code is written during the event, and repository history may be audited. The treatment of pre-event material is still unclarified by the organizers.
