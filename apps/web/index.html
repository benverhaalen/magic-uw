Daily agenda

Your day, ranked by what matters.

Code orders everything due by how little time it leaves you, so the first item is the one that can't wait. When an announcement moves a date, the agenda changes and quotes your professor's own words.

Study spaces

Every item gets its own study space.

Open an exam, problem set or reading and it's already connected: the lecture, the syllabus rule and the real due date, each with the line it came from.

Practice

Practice that points back to its source.

One click builds a practice test from your own course materials. Every answer links to the passage it came from, and code checks each quote before you see it.

Course analytics

See where you stand, and what to study next.

Each course shows your grade trend, homework completion, readiness for each assessment and topic mastery, with the next thing to do. Mastery comes from your practice, and it is never a grade prediction.

Privacy

Whiz guards the gate.

  • Stays on your laptop. Your courses are stored on your device.
  • Read-only. It never submits, posts or enrolls you in anything.
  • The AI gets no tools. Inside the app it answers one checked request at a time and can't act.
  • Every quote checked. Code verifies each quote, date and ID against your materials.
